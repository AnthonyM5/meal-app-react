_Audit-plan AC #3 (`NUTRIENT_API_SOURCING_AND_AUDIT.md` §3.2, corpus-coverage portion). Generated 2026-07-08._

## Corpus coverage

How much of the USDA whole-food corpus relevant to fresh feeding the app has actually imported. Totals from `/fdc/v1/foods/search?query=*&pageSize=1&dataType=<type>` (`totalHits`).

| Data type | Available in FDC | Imported into `foods` |
|---|---|---|
| Foundation | 394 | (see note) |
| SR Legacy | 7793 | (see note) |
| **Total (Foundation + SR Legacy)** | **8187** | **19** |

**Corpus coverage: 19 / 8187 = 0.23%.**

> Note: the `foods` table does not persist USDA `dataType`, so the per-type imported split isn't recorded. All current USDA rows were sourced from SR Legacy queries (muscle meats, organs, eggs, fish, staple starches). Against SR Legacy alone the figure is 19/7793 = 0.24%.

## Interpretation

This is expected and **by design, not a defect.** Import is query-triggered (`/api/ingredients/import`, `scripts/import-cooked-ingredients.ts`, `scripts/018_import_raw_counterparts.ts`) and curated toward canine fresh-feeding staples, not a corpus-wide bulk load. The vast majority of FDC SR Legacy is human-oriented (processed foods, restaurant items, brand-name products, prepared dishes) with no place in a fresh dog bowl. A high corpus-coverage percentage is **not** a goal here — targeted coverage of the ingredients dogs actually eat is.

### Note on the quinoa flag (classifier limitation, not a data gap)

FDC labels dry quinoa **"Quinoa, uncooked"**, and `inferPreparationState()` only matches
the whole word `raw` — so "uncooked" classifies as `null`, reading as a missing-raw
variant. Two takeaways:

- **Not a real gap**: dogs are fed *cooked* quinoa; the raw/dry form is irrelevant to a
  bowl. Quinoa's `expect` could legitimately be `cooked_only`.
- **Real, minor finding**: `inferPreparationState()` doesn't recognize `uncooked`/`dried`/
  `dry` grain terminology. Optional low-risk improvement — add `uncooked` to the raw
  pattern — but it touches the shared import classifier, so it's flagged here rather than
  changed as part of a read-only audit. `dried` is deliberately *not* auto-added (dried
  fruit ≠ raw).
